**User-facing fix:** users west of UTC (all of the Americas) and users of Thai or Arabic display cultures see their real BIOS release date. Today they see the previous day, a Buddhist-era year (2569) or a Hijri date. A user with a completely full disk sees `0` GB free instead of "Not available". 3. **WR-02 (SPEC-04):** `Akari.ps1` ~lines 294 and 308 count the volume Label as a fallible field, so Disk `_Status` is `Partial` on healthy machines.

Purpose: the project's core value is "accurate, live system specs; if the specs are wrong ... the page fails its purpose." A wrong date presented as fact is a blocker even though the fix is one line.
